Air Quality Forecasting in Montréal

Julia · 01/2026 – 04/2026 · team of 5 · 95/100 · MTH3302

Forecast the daily concentration of fine particulate matter (PM) across 7 Montréal stations for all 365 days of 2025, i.e. 2,555 predictions, from 17 years of data.

  • Air + weather data join, exploratory analysis, 20+ engineered features (rolling means, sine/cosine seasonality, temperature × month interactions)
  • Missing values handled per variable; temporal split validation, never random
  • 4 variants compared; selected model: linear regression (GLM.jl, 23 variables) with no feature built from past PM, to avoid error propagation
  • RMSE of 18.25 on Kaggle; limitations documented: underestimation of wildfire smoke episodes
  • Time series
  • Linear regression
  • Feature engineering

Graph Clustering by Modularity Maximization (VNS)

Julia · 07/2026 – present · INF8111 Data Mining

Step-by-step reimplementation of the pipeline from Aloise et al. (10th DIMACS Implementation Challenge), a paper suggested by Professor Daniel Aloise.

  • LPA → LPAm → LPAm+ → VNS/VNDS: each step escapes the local optima of the previous one
  • 9 benchmark graphs, from 34 to over 31,000 vertices, 40 runs per method and graph (seeds 1 to 40)
  • Comparison with the literature, review of 5 scientific papers
  • Julia chosen for near-compiled speed with Python-like readability
  • Graph theory
  • Metaheuristics
  • Optimization
